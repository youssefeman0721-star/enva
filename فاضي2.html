We protect the environment

because it is the fundamental cradle for human life and all living organisms, and because it ensures the continued existence of the foundations for a decent life. Key reasons for protecting the environment: • Protecting public health: Reducing air, water, and soil pollution limits the spread of chronic diseases such as asthma, heart attacks, and respiratory conditions. • Ensuring resources for future generations: Using natural resources wisely guarantees the availability of clean water, fertile soil, and forests for the future. • Combating climate change: Lowering greenhouse gas and carbon emissions helps curb global warming and reduce the occurrence of natural disasters. • Protecting biodiversity: Preventing the extinction of animals and plants, and maintaining the balance of natural ecosystems. • Economic viability: Protecting the environment and reducing pollution saves billions of dollars that would otherwise be spent on healthcare and addressing the damage caused by pollution.
